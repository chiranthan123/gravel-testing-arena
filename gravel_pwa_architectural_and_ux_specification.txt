1. CORE ARCHITECTURAL PHILOSOPHY: "EVERYTHING IS AN INDEXED FEATURE"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Application Name : Gravel
* Platform Type    : Offline-First Progressive Web Application (PWA)
* Core Paradigm    : Feature-Indexed Micro-Module Architecture
* Primary Domain   : NIOS (National Institute of Open Schooling) Institutions, 
                     with zero-code Dev reconfigurability for Colleges, 
                     Coaching/Tuition Centres, and Standard K-12 Schools.

1.1 How the Feature-Index Engine Works
In Gravel, every mechanism—including UI shells, navigation bars, form field 
schemas, table filters, mathematical formulas, charts, and role workflows—is 
encapsulated as an independent module with a unique **Feature Index ID** 
(e.g., `[F-UI-001]`, `[F-DAT-103]`, `[F-MATH-304]`).

Instead of hardcoding pages for a single institution type, the application 
reads a **Client Manifest Blueprint** controlled by the `DEV` account. The 
application structure acts as a skeletal layout tree where **Feature Indices** 
are mounted into specific slots.

  [Tenant Manifest (JSON)] ---> [Feature Registry Lookup] ---> [Rendered UI & DB Schema]

1.2 Multi-Institution Adaptability (Dev-Controlled)
By toggling or swapping Feature Indices in the Dev Console, a Developer can 
instantly morph Gravel across different educational models:
* Mode A: NIOS Accredited Institution (AI) / Study Centre (Default Focus)
  Mounts NIOS Stream/Block schemas, TMA (Tutor Marked Assignments), PCP 
  (Personal Contact Programmes), TOC (Transfer of Credit), and On-Demand Exam 
  (ODE) vs. Public Exam trackers.
* Mode B: Coaching / Tuition Institute
  Swaps NIOS schemas for Competitive Target Exams, Batch Mock-Test Ranks, 
  Fee Installment Trackers, and Doubt-Session Booking modules.
* Mode C: Degree College / University Department
  Swaps in Semester/Trimester Credit Systems, SGPA/CGPA Calculators, Lab 
  Internal Assessments, and Elective Course Registration modules.

CATEGORY 04: MATHEMATICAL & STATISTICAL ANALYTICS ENGINE [F-MATH-xxx]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All calculations execute automatically based on active observation filters.

* [F-MATH-301] Weighted Attendance Rate ($AR$) Calculator
  Computes overall, subject-wise, or PCP attendance percentage:
  $$AR = \left( \frac{N_{\text{present}} + w \cdot N_{\text{late}}}{N_{\text{total}}} \right) \times 100\%$$
  Where $w \in [0, 1]$ is the configurable weight for late arrivals (default $w = 0.5$).

* [F-MATH-302] Attendance Safe-Margin & Recovery Calculator
  Calculates how many classes a student can safely miss ($M_{\text{safe}}$) or 
  must consecutively attend ($C_{\text{req}}$) to maintain target threshold $T$ 
  (e.g., $T = 0.75$ for general or mandatory PCP completion):
  $$M_{\text{safe}} = \left\lfloor \frac{N_{\text{present}} - T \cdot N_{\text{total}}}{T} \right\rfloor, \quad C_{\text{req}} = \left\lceil \frac{T \cdot N_{\text{total}} - N_{\text{present}}}{1 - T} \right\rceil$$

* [F-MATH-303] NIOS Composite Subject Score & TMA Weightage Engine
  Computes the projected or final NIOS subject score ($S_{\text{final}}$) 
  combining Theory Exam marks, Practical marks, and TMA (20% of Theory max):
  - For Non-Practical Subjects (Max Theory $T_{\max} = 100$, scaled to $80$ + TMA $20$):
    $$S_{\text{final}} = \left( \frac{M_{\text{theory}}}{100} \times 80 \right) + M_{\text{TMA}}$$
  - For Practical Subjects (e.g., Max Theory $80$ scaled to $64$, Practical $20$, TMA $16$):
    $$S_{\text{final}} = \left( \frac{M_{\text{theory}}}{80} \times 64 \right) + M_{\text{TMA (scaled to 16)}} + M_{\text{practical}}$$
  - Enforces NIOS Passing Criteria: $\ge 33\%$ in aggregate AND $\ge 33\%$ 
    separately in Theory and Practical components.

* [F-MATH-304] NIOS 5-Session Formative PCP Score Aggregator
  Calculates Practical PCP formative assessment marks across the 5 mandatory 
  practical contact sessions:
  $$M_{\text{PCP-Prac}} = \sum_{k=1}^{5} p_k$$

* [F-MATH-305] Cohort Mean ($\mu$) & Standard Deviation ($\sigma$) Engine
  Measures central tendency and score dispersion per subject, batch, or exam:
  $$\mu = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i, \quad \sigma = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (x_i - \mu)^2}$$

* [F-MATH-306] Student Percentile Rank ($PR$) Calculator
  Determines relative standing within a batch, stream, or mock test series:
  $$PR = \left( \frac{L + 0.5 \cdot S}{N} \right) \times 100$$
  Where $L$ is count of scores below $x$, $S$ is count equal to $x$, and $N$ is 
  cohort size.

* [F-MATH-307] Attendance-to-Performance Pearson Correlation ($r$)
  Quantifies how strongly class/PCP attendance correlates with test scores:
  $$r = \frac{\sum (AR_i - \overline{AR})(x_i - \mu)}{\sqrt{\sum (AR_i - \overline{AR})^2 \sum (x_i - \mu)^2}}$$

* [F-MATH-308] College Credit & SGPA/CGPA Engine (Multi-Institution Option)
  Computes Semester Grade Point Average for college deployments:
  $$\text{SGPA} = \frac{\sum_{j=1}^{m} (C_j \cdot G_j)}{\sum_{j=1}^{m} C_j}$$
  Where $C_j$ is course credit and $G_j$ is grade point earned.
